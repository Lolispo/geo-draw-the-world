Flag colours

data/flags.json is hand-curated and drives both colour quizzes, so a wrong entry is scored against. This reads the actual flag image for every entity and checks whether each curated colour appears in it. The 52 entities that had no colours were drafted here and landed after review, so "No colours yet" should now be empty — if an entry appears there, a new entity has arrived without colours. Nothing here writes to the repo; drafts are proposals to paste in.

Calibrated against the 199 flags already curated, the reader finds 80% of their colours and errs towards extra anti-aliasing shades rather than missing real ones. So "disagrees" means worth looking at, not wrong: a small device or a thin stripe can fall under the threshold. Read it as a worklist, and trust the flag beside it over the swatches.
